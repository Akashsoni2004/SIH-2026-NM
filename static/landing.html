NATIONAL CONTEXT

Bridging the Gap Between Project Data and Actionable Intelligence

Central infrastructure projects carry thousands of crores in public investment. Manual review of massive monthly reports inevitably creates blind spots where delays compound before intervention occurs.

The Traditional Challenge

  • Data Overload: Hundreds of pages of static Flash Reports generated each month.
  • Retrospective Analysis: Delays only identified after deadlines have already passed.
  • Siloed Monitoring: Disconnected spreadsheets across 15+ ministries and 40+ agencies.
  • Hidden Cost Drift: Gradual expenditure escalation without ground physical progress.

The PAIMANA AI Solution

  • Longitudinal Intelligence: 4-month velocity tracking ($\Delta$Progress/mo & $\Delta$Spend/mo).
  • Predictive Forecasts: Ahead-of-time delay slippage & final cost estimation.
  • Explainable AI (XAI): Transparent SHAP driver attribution for every project risk score.
  • Human-in-the-Loop: Actionable advisory recommendations empowering authorized officials.
CORE CAPABILITIES

Comprehensive Multi-Model Monitoring Suite

Eight purpose-built modules designed for the Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation.

Performance Dashboard

Real-time macro portfolio telemetry tracking 1,737 infrastructure projects with ministry and sector aggregations.

Risk Prediction Engine

Stratified XGBoost classifier achieving 90.52% accuracy categorizing projects into HIGH, MEDIUM, and LOW risk.

Schedule Delay Regressor

High-precision ML regressor predicting timeline slippages in months and calendar days with a 4.28-month MAE.

Cost Overrun Forecaster

Anticipates price escalation percentages and forecasts final projected capital requirements upon completion.

Early Warning Alerts

Isolation Forest anomaly engine detecting critical imbalances (e.g. 85% budget spent with 30% physical progress).

AI Decision Recommendations

Prescriptive advisory measures providing administrative steps, milestone recovery plans, and audit advisories.

Longitudinal Velocity Analysis

Calculates true ground execution rates and fund burn velocities across consecutive monthly Flash Report cycles.

Multi-Sector Governance

Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) with organization-level data isolation for NHAI, AAI, Railways, and Central Ministries.
