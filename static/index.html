Why XGBoost Won?

Tabular government data has non-linear thresholds (e.g. delay >12m, high progress-spend gap). Gradient boosting captures sharp multi-dimensional boundaries better than linear models while avoiding overfitting via L1/L2 regularization.

Anomaly Detection Method

Isolation Forest isolates non-conforming projects in sub-dimensional trees based on expenditure burn velocity, progress velocity, and progress gaps without requiring prior anomaly labels.
